ABOUT THE GAME

Rookie Reaper is a top-down RPG made for android, where you play as a reaper on his first day on the job!

The Mission: Find and Harvest five souls from immortals spread across the land.

The Combat: A Soulslite experience, fight strong foes, learn their moves and act accordingly.

The Customization: Find your own way to play, level your stats, change your weapon, skills and visual in a way that matches your playstyle.

Rookie Reaper - Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Euron Cross collects, uses, and discloses your information in connection with your use of our mobile game, Rookie Reaper.

Information We Collect

We collect very limited information from you in connection with the Game. We utilize the Google Play Games Saved Games API to allow you to save your game progress. This API stores your save data on your own Google Drive account. We do not access or store any of your save data ourselves.

Use of Information

We do not use the information collected through the Google Play Games Saved Games API for any purpose other than enabling you to save and load your game progress.

Sharing of Information

We do not share any information collected through the Game with any third parties. Your save data is stored on your own Google Drive and is not accessible by us.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information collected through the Game from loss, theft, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method is 100% secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this h3rivacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on the Google Play Store. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Your Consent
